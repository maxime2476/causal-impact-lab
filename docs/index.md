# Causal Impact Lab

Estimating the **causal effect of contractionary US monetary policy shocks on
US employment**, with intellectual honesty as the primary deliverable.

Two estimands, in an explicit hierarchy:

1. **Headline — relative effect (cleanly identified).** Do regions/industries
   with greater interest-rate exposure lose more employment after an identified
   contractionary monetary shock? Identified off cross-sectional heterogeneity,
   with time fixed effects absorbing all aggregate/common shocks.
2. **Complement — aggregate dynamic effect (assumption-dependent).** The impulse
   response of national employment to an identified shock, reported separately
   with its identifying assumptions stated and stress-tested.
